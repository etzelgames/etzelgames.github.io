Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26.11.2026

This policy explains how Tower Site (the "Game"), developed by Etzel Games (Faruk Kayık, Türkiye) ("we", the data controller), handles data. It is written with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Turkish data protection law (KVKK) in mind.

1. Accounts and personal information

The Game does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address or phone number.

2. Data stored on your device

Level progress, stars and settings (language, sound, vibration, night mode) are stored only on your device and are not sent to us. You can delete them with Settings > "Reset progress" or by uninstalling the Game.

3. Advertising

The Game is free and shows Google AdMob ads. AdMob may process data such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app usage information to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and can change their choice at any time in Settings > "Ad & privacy choices". You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. More: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4. Game statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the Game is played and to improve it, we use Google Analytics for Firebase, a service provided by Google. It records gameplay events (such as level started, level completed or failed, stars earned and use of the undo option) together with technical data such as an app instance ID, advertising ID, device model, operating system, app version, session information and approximate location (country or city) derived from your IP address. This data does not include your name or contact details and we do not use it to identify you. Google processes this data on our behalf and it may be stored on servers outside Türkiye (for example in the USA). Statistics are deleted automatically after at most 14 months. More: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete and object to the processing of your data. The Game does not collect your name or contact details; statistics and advertising data are linked only to device identifiers, which you can reset or delete in your Android settings. For such data you can also contact Google. If you email us, your email address and message are used only to reply to you, are not shared with third parties and are deleted on request.

7. Changes and contact

If the Game is sold or transferred to another company, the data described in this policy may be transferred to the new owner, who will continue to handle it under this policy or inform you of any changes. We may update this policy; the current version is always available in the Game and at etzelgames.github.io/towersite/privacy/. Questions: etzelgames@gmail.com
